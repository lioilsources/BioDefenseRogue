Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

This policy covers ImmunoRun, an action roguelite about the immune system with the illustrated book Lexikon zdraví, by Oldřich Vořechovský / A+B, on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web. We do not collect personal information. To learn where the game is too hard or confusing, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics, described below, which you can turn off at any time.

Anonymous Gameplay Statistics

What is sent. Short records of gameplay moments: the app was opened or went to the background, the main menu was ready (and how many milliseconds that took), which game screen was shown and for how many seconds (menu, run, pause, upgrade choice, door choice, result, book), a run started or ended (how it ended, seconds played, which room it reached, what ended it, rooms cleared, upgrades taken, memory cells earned), which upgrade card or door was picked, and whether the book was opened, a quiz finished, the font size changed, the page for parents opened or the sound switched off. Each record also carries the app version, platform (for example iOS or Windows), app language and two coarse bands: roughly how many times the game has been played on this device (1, 2, 3–5, 6–10, 11+) and roughly how many days ago (0, 1, 2–6, 7–29, 30+). Nothing from inside the book is sent: no chapter, no page and no quiz answer. The full list is published in the game's source repository (docs/telemetry.md). The web version of the game sends nothing.

Why. To see how many new players get from launch to their first finished run, how long sessions last, where runs end and whether the book is used, so we can make the game and the book better.

No identifiers. The statistics contain no name, no email, no text of any kind, no account, no device or advertising identifier and no exact device model. Each play session gets a random number that exists only while the app runs, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold or shared.

Who receives them, and where. The statistics are received and stored by the developer, on the developer's own server in the EU (Czech Republic). Nobody else receives them: no analytics company or other third party is involved. Sending them, like any internet connection, reveals your IP address to that server; the IP address is not stored, it is used only to derive your country. Your country is the only location information kept.

How long. Statistics are kept for 12 months and then deleted.

Offline. The game works fully offline. Without a connection, up to 500 recent records wait on your device and are sent later; older ones are discarded.

How to turn them off. In the main menu, select STATS: ON so it reads STATS: OFF (below the sound switch; the label is translated into your language, in Czech STATISTIKY). This takes effect immediately: records waiting on your device are deleted and nothing more is sent, until you turn it back on.

Data We Do Not Collect

ImmunoRun does not collect:

— Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account)

— Anything you type, or your answers in the book's quizzes

— Precise location (only the country, as above)

— Device identifiers, the advertising ID or the exact device model

— Contacts, photos, files or anything outside the game

Local Storage

Game progress (memory cells, runs, the last cause of death for vaccination, book stamps, the last chapter, finished quizzes and the book font size) is stored locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults). We never receive it. The statistics keep a few small values there — the day measuring began, a play-session counter and the last day band — from which the bands above are worked out on your device.

Third-Party Services

ImmunoRun contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, no accounts and no in-app purchases. The anonymous statistics go only to the developer's own server, as described above.

The page for parents and teachers in the book lists a few public health websites. They open in your browser only after you confirm; what those websites collect is governed by their own policies.

Children

ImmunoRun is made for players from 10 years old and for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: the anonymous statistics described above contain nothing that identifies a person, and they can be turned off in the main menu. Parents can read the same summary on the page for parents in the book.
